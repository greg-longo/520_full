Instructor preview

Fills in the progress the site gates on, so you can walk through the capstone and the finished dashboards without playing every simulation first.

This writes to this browser only. Progress that gates the site is kept in localStorage, not on your account - so this is per-browser and per-device, and signing in elsewhere will not carry it. It also does not send anything to the server, so it will not appear in the Events sheet or affect any real completion data.

What this does not do

It fills the twelve simulation keys the capstone gate reads. It does not fabricate capstone progress itself - open the commission and play it, or use the tracker's own empty state. And it is not a security boundary: any client-side unlock is readable by anyone who opens the page source. It is a convenience for you, not a lock for them. The page is noindex and linked from nowhere, which is the extent of it.
