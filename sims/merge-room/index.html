The Merge Room

Four systems, one artist roster, and no two of them agree.

Vantage Records keeps its roster in four places. artists is the label's own list. contracts comes from legal. streams is what the streaming platform reports back. royalties is what finance actually paid out.

You have been asked for one number: the average royalty payment per artist, for a press release about how well Vantage treats its musicians. It sounds like a one-line answer. It is a one-line answer that is wrong in four different ways, and the whole job is finding out how.

Work through four checks. Each one is a few lines of pandas, and each one changes the number. At the end you will be asked to defend the figure you would actually publish.

Nothing here is graded on speed. Getting a wrong answer and understanding why is the exercise.
