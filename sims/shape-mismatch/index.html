Every other lab hands you a blank editor. This one hands you code that is already written and already wrong, along with the error Python gave it.

You have to say what the shapes are before you may edit anything. That is the whole point. Almost every NumPy error you will meet names both shapes in its message, and the fix is obvious the moment you have actually read them - which is the step everyone skips.

Five points a level: three for reading it correctly, two for the fix. You can skip straight to fixing if you already see it, and score one.
