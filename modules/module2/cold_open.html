Question one

2.52mean undergraduate GPA

Question two

Who does that average describe?

Nobody in this cohort is average. The number was not a good representation of the data.

Question three

Does anything students do seem to move the outcome?

Now you are not describing a column. You are comparing groups.

Question four

Of everything we record, what tracks success most closely?

What a student does here outranks what they arrived holding. That is a finding, and it is one you could hand to a Cabinet.

Question five

What about the students the data forgot?

The missing rows are not a random sample. They belong to the students you most need to find. Delete those rows and you delete the problem.

Question six

Should the University fund high school club programs?

One of these two numbers is misrepresenting information about clubs. By the end of this course you will know which one, and it takes about four lines of code to show it.
